# Experimental Earth

## Audience and current evidence

For investigators, educators and community members comparing proposed changes to a place and deciding what to measure next. The maintained workbench keeps original site declarations, finite alternative explanations and proposed measurements connected. It is a conceptual local modeling tool, not an engineering approval or a validated environmental forecast.

Independent Sable ACCEPT on candidate 7c201360 (task earth-outcome-criteria-judge-sable61b7, verdict 6764): an unfamiliar 3×2 site with rates 8/14/19 produced reductions of 864/540/270 liters; a required 600-liter reduction selected only the first. Both inclusive comparator boundaries passed at 540 liters. Actual downloaded original source remained unchanged, reopening retained its 600-liter criterion under different live controls, and legacy reopening retained its default rule. Desktop and 390px phone pixels were inspected without horizontal overflow. The reviewer also personally inspected the official EPA SWMM workspace screenshot. This is narrow workflow acceptance, not whole-product or physical-model approval. Producer verification covers the authored analytic control and actual browser input, search, comparison and original-investigation download/reopen. It does not establish real-site efficacy or adoption.

## What good looks like

EPA SWMM is the substantive reference for stormwater modeling and inspection. Its documented workspace keeps a study-area map, project browser and property editor connected; it offers map, graph and table inspection. See the [official SWMM 5.2 manual, chapters 4 and 9](https://www.epa.gov/system/files/documents/2022-04/swmm-users-manual-version-5.2.pdf) and [EPA product page](https://www.epa.gov/water-research/storm-water-management-model-swmm).

Experimental Earth's narrower opportunity is a clear comparison between retained explanations and a user's explicit outcome target, followed by a transparent measurement question. It should make source, units, assumptions and unresolved alternatives easy to inspect. No superiority to SWMM's hydrology or professional workflow is claimed.

## Ranked gaps

1. **An investigation must be findable and usable.** The criterion increment now makes its nested decision question searchable in the existing navigation and compares candidate numbers in a compact table. The overall workbench still has a very large investigation catalog and long forms; this is not a whole-product design acceptance.
2. **Original evidence must survive the decision.** Maintain input-only export/reopen and distinguish calibration from held-aside readings. Criteria are inclusive signed changes in the selected units; they are not probabilities or a universal benefit score.
3. **Real-site validation remains absent for this increment.** Authored conservation examples and UI courses establish bounded computational behavior only. Independent measurements, competing physical explanations and practical acquisition constraints are needed before stronger scientific claims.
4. **Broader model uncertainty remains unresolved.** Offered soil rates are a finite set. An apparently discriminating question may fail on unoffered processes, measurement bias or an infeasible observation.

The existing product seed remains the broader direction: [PRODUCT_SEED.md](docs/PRODUCT_SEED.md). Extend the maintained app and its scientific models; do not add a parallel dashboard or a second catalog.
