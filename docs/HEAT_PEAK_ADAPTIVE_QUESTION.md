## Finite minimax mechanism

At any possible first value, retain all models whose original guarded band includes it. For every chronological second query, compute the maintained worst remaining target-peak spread over that active family; choose its minimum. Choose the first query minimizing the worst of those conditional minima. The fair fixed comparison reuses the identical endpoint-by-second-query evaluations: for each fixed second query take its maximum over first endpoints, then choose the minimum over all chronological first/second pairs. No extra finitePeakQuestion calls are made. Ties retain the original first/second query traversal order.

All distinct first-band endpoints suffice for this worst-case calculation. Every open interval between endpoints has an active family contained in an adjacent closed-endpoint family. For a fixed second query its worst peak spread is monotone under family inclusion, and the minimum over the same second-query choices is also monotone. Thus no open-cell value exceeds the checked endpoint maximum. The actual entered value is filtered directly, including values between endpoints; the consumer does not interpolate a posterior or choose a nearest endpoint.

The same complete family and rectangular guarded reading bands are retained. No probability distribution or independent-error assumption is introduced. Earlier exposure-time queries cannot be selected after a later first reading. An earlier research prototype allowed that retrospective operation; its results were retained separately and were not promoted as prospective acquisition evidence.
