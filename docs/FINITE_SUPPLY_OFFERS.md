# Choose an offered finite-delivery soil test

The offered-test section of `ponded-uptake.html` compares explicitly supplied delivery protocols and surface-reading times. Start with an original ponded study, then declare the separate offered schedules. Importing that study leaves acquisition basis and offers empty. The fictional example supplies three authored choices: low supply leaves both original explanations dry, a stronger offered test separates them, and an unavailable stronger test cannot be chosen.

Each offer contains identity, source basis, availability, one positive covered reading time, future surface-volume measurement half-width and the complete finite-delivery protocol. Explicit original nominal soil-state and area reuse, initial surface water and no-other-loss assumptions are required for each separate test. It is not a continuation of an actual finite-delivery course. Rate and initial-water uncertainty are not modeled.
