# Carry inferred receiver families into the actual proposal comparison

An investigator can now infer receiving storage, initial water and drainage from original measurements and immediately compare the existing and proposal layouts across the complete generated calibration-compatible family. The earlier inverse displayed parameters and future measurement questions; this consumer carries those parameters through the maintained paired water calculation without manual transcription or selecting a single explanation.

In **Infer receivers without supplying their initial water**, copy or reopen the original receiving account. Choose analytic proposals or the original-band search plus retained analytic proposals, explain why the same per-cell receiver and initial water apply to this material in both layouts, then **Infer and compare the complete family**.

The authored inferred-bed example follows that complete path. Its original analytic proposals all contradict at least one original calibration band; the band search retains three calibration-compatible receivers. All three are applied to both copied layouts. In this four-square-metre control, the sealed original retains 72 L of surface water at event end and the hypothetical garden layout retains zero. Each fitted receiver therefore predicts a −72 L surface-water difference. Peak-depth reduction varies slightly across the three explanations, approximately 13.9972–14.0005 mm.

Consequently the receiver's cumulative drainage can exceed the event's 120 L rainfall; it must not be reported as rainwater capture or a measured benefit. These are authored model controls, not environmental field results.

## Exact evidence and family behavior

Only the original selected evidence scenario calibrates the inverse. Its name may be `existing` or `proposal`; it is shown explicitly. The opposite layout supplies no fitting observations. Each inferred parameter combination is frozen before comparison, then the same combination is passed unchanged to both original maps with the same original weather.

Original-band mode retains all calibration-compatible analytic proposals and all calibration-compatible numerical witnesses. Exact numerical duplicates are compared once and retain every analytic/search origin. There is no first-model choice, held-best selection, family truncation, probability assignment or continuous feasible-region claim.

The consumer reuses the existing `compareDrainingLayer` unchanged. This performs its maintained six runs per receiver: unlimited, closed and draining assumptions on each original layout. The paired draining run on the evidence scenario is independently checked against every original surface and whole-receiver calibration band before any comparison is accepted. A disagreement refuses the complete result. The maintained unlimited and closed counterpart changes remain in the report as separate context; they do not replace a rejected inferred receiver.

All calibration-compatible models remain in the principal consequence range, including models contradicted by held-aside readings. A second clearly labelled range summarizes those also compatible with held-aside readings. Held values never fit parameters, choose which models are compared, change their effects or select prospective questions. If every held course fails, the adverse status stays visible with the original conditional family. If calibration supports none, the range is absent rather than zero or a fallback result.

The displayed differences are **proposal minus existing** for water entering the ground, final surface water, surface-boundary outflow, peak depth, initial receiver inventory, final receiver inventory and cumulative receiver drainage. Individual models expose both original values, differences, counts of receiving cells, balance errors and original receiving readings. The material-transfer declaration is explicit because a surface label alone does not establish identical soil, initial moisture or boundary conditions across layouts.

## Qualification

Eight native behavioral groups pass:

- Independently calculated four-cell surface accounting, both actual maintained maps, separate initial below-ground water and receiver conservation.
- Complete analytic/search union and exact-duplicate origin retention.
- Wrong held values preserve every fitted parameter, compared model and predicted change while rejecting the held result.
- Identical layouts give zero changes; swapping layouts and evidence scenario reverses every change; other-scenario readings do not refit the selected footprint.
- Contradictory original calibration produces no consequence range or default receiver.
- Complete combined-work refusal and missing transfer basis run zero producers and zero paired comparisons.
- Deliberately corrupted paired receiving output cannot inherit success from the earlier fit.
- Strict source-only replay and refusal of saved parameters/effects/verdicts or an unsupported selection mode.
