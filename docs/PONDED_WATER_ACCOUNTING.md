# Account for original water before comparing soil explanations

The ponded test consumes net-infiltrated water, not supplied water alone. The water-accounting panel retains four sourced quantities over one declared test volume and cumulative interval:

- All water entering the test volume, including any rain or other inflow that occurred.
- Initial surface-water inventory.
- Final surface-water inventory.
- Other non-infiltration losses, including evaporation, leakage and lateral outflow.

Net volume equals incoming water plus initial inventory minus final inventory and other losses. Each original term is either bounded with its original L/mL quantity and uncertainty, or explicitly unknown with a source explanation. Missing terms refuse; unknown terms never become zero. A declared zero needs its own source. The fictional example is expressly authored, not a measurement protocol or field observation.

Conversion and sum/subtraction allowances remain separate from declared measurement bounds.
