# Compare receiving mechanisms with original water observations

The Rain Lab capacity comparison now offers an explicit draining-layer continuation. Supply total receiving capacity in mm, initially stored water in mm, drainage coefficient per hour and the original source or hypothetical basis. The comparison retains unlimited and closed-store alternatives and tests every declared rainfall, duration, soil-rate and receiver combination. It calls the maintained rainfall solver with its existing draining-layer model and surface-rate override; no new water solver is introduced.

Calibration alone admits and orders explanations. Held-aside water and rainfall readings only challenge them. A draining reservoir is a declared model, not evidence of groundwater behavior. Equal predictions remain separate alternatives. No result edits the active site or adopts a physical cause.

## Capability control

For authored uniform rain 30 mm/h, infiltration rate 20 mm/h, total capacity 5 mm, initially empty and drainage coefficient 1/hour, storage fills at ln(20/15) hours. Before filling, uptake is 20t; thereafter it is 20 ln(20/15) + 5(t-ln(20/15)). The storm ends at minute 60. At minutes 7 and 63 a suitably chosen closed-store alternative fits the original calibration alongside the draining model. The held-aside reading at minute 126 rejects that closed-store explanation. Changing only that held-aside reading can instead favor the closed store without changing calibration support. Setting drainage to zero reproduces the closed-store limit and retains both labels.

The complete comparison reserves at most 3,000,000 cell steps before fitting, including all baseline and draining families. One to eight distinct, explicitly sourced draining offers are admitted through maintained layer validation. Original-only JSON transfer recomputes on reopen; injected reports are refused. Invalid replacement and input edits retire accepted output. A detached parent cannot publish a late file result.

This is finite offered-model comparison. It does not establish uniqueness beyond the offered family, infer arbitrary rainfall histories, measure groundwater, or estimate probabilities.

## Remaining ambiguity

An additional negative control admits two draining stores: capacity 5, coefficient 1/hour and initial storage 0; capacity 10, coefficient 0.5/hour and initial storage `(20 − 15 exp(0.5 ln(20/15)))/0.5`. Both have saturated throughput 5 mm/h and the same filling time under infiltration rate 20 mm/h. The original water readings cannot distinguish them, and both survive. A dry-tail separation from a closed store does not establish unique drainage parameters or initial storage.
