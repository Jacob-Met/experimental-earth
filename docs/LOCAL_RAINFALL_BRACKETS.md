# Complementary local rainfall brackets

The original two-anchor proposal follows one bounded nested path. Routed depths can produce several locally bracketed calibration paths; the endpoints of the full duration range need not expose every one. Finite failure of the original path therefore does not exclude a mechanism.

The explicit complementary policy retains the complete original native-prefix course. It then samples nine evenly spaced duration slices, including the declared endpoints. At each slice it reuses the maintained rainfall center-proposal routine for the earlier original calibration anchor. Adjacent slice representatives whose later-anchor residual signs differ, or have a zero endpoint residual, form local proposal brackets. This is a proposal condition, not proof of continuity, monotonicity, existence or uniqueness of a root.

At most two brackets are refined. Their deterministic priority is the maximum absolute earlier-anchor endpoint residual plus the maximum absolute later-anchor endpoint residual; both are measured in mm. Equal priorities use original interval order. This priority allocates a finite numerical budget; it does not rank physical mechanisms or establish that unselected brackets are inferior. Every sampled slice, bracket, selected status and attempted point remains available. Brackets without a search status are unresolved. Held-aside values never select brackets, guide proposals or change calibration acceptance.

The same native model dispatch and original-clock prefix method receive every proposed point. Complete original depth, interval-gauge and shared-baseline cumulative-counter constraints still decide compatibility. Held-aside originals only challenge the resulting family. No working-site parameters, measurements or mechanisms are adopted.

## Work and transfer

For declared iteration depth `d`, let `n = d + 2`. Each original model reserves the original `n²` point course plus `9n + 2n²` additional native-prefix evaluations. The existing baseline paired model runs and original-offer prefix receiving are also reserved. The complete sum is checked against the unchanged three-million cell-step and 200,000 evidence-row caps before any inference. Cache reuse can reduce actual work; it never reduces the reservation.

The policy requires an explicit basis and the native-prefix reading method. The original nested policy remains the default. Source-only schema `experimental-earth-mechanism-forcing-search/3` records original inputs, numerical method, iteration depth and policy. Reopening recomputes them; older readers refuse the new schema. Reports and proposed parameter values are not imported as evidence.

## Executed coverage and limits

A fixed-seed experiment generated 24 unfamiliar two-by-two routed pressure courses using the maintained native solver. The original finite search missed ten generating mechanisms. Sampling local duration brackets recovered three of those misses; seven remained unresolved. These authored native-generated inputs test proposal coverage, not independent solver accuracy or field validity.

A separate fresh-seed twelve-course full-family qualification, not used to choose the policy, retains the generating mechanism in seven courses under the original policy and eight under the complementary policy; four remain unresolved. The generated native controls still test finite proposal coverage only.

Three recovered courses and an unresolved negative are retained as original test fixtures. With iteration depth19, the first complete finite family changes from59 calibration-compatible points and zero held-aside survivors to210 compatible points and49 survivors, reserving2,785,360 cell steps. Every original attempted point remains an exact prefix of its corresponding new course. Raising depth to21 refuses the complete course under the same cap. Contradictory held-aside readings preserve every tested coordinate and calibration decision while eliminating survivors.

Finite surviving points do not cover a continuous region or identify a physical cause. Numerical prefix evaluations remain separate replays, not a jointly refined trajectory or a global numerical error certificate.
