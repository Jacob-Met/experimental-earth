04 / Interpretation

What this sheet can and cannot say

Supported: whole-site modeled surface-water totals from the existing simulator, sampled at identical retained model minutes for two proposals over one unchanged baseline.

Not supported: calibrated flood risk, actual measurements, continuous-time crossing dates, real-world superiority, local-cell consequences or a causal comparison of different weather or original surfaces.
