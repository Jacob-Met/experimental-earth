When this comparison applies

Each batch is an explicit idealization as an instantaneous addition. Finite pouring duration, unrecorded refills, overflow, rainfall, evaporation and leakage need another protocol. A measured head value or a final water total cannot supply the missing delivery history.

The homogeneous wetting front keeps its accumulated infiltration through dry gaps. Zero moisture deficit selects a mathematical control that neglects head; when A is positive, it does not imply a physically dry deficit of zero. This local comparison does not adopt properties into the site or establish field accuracy.
